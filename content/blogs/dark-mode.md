A common mistake when designing dark mode is grabbing pure black (`#000000`) and pure white (`#ffffff`). But stare at that for too long, and your eyes will scream.

### The Notion Approach

Notion uses `#191919` for their dark mode background. It's soft enough to reduce eye strain, while still conveying a premium, document-like feel.

*   **Background**: `#191919`
*   **Text**: `#ebeced`
*   **Muted Text**: `#9b9a97`

By mimicking these exact variables, this portfolio instantly feels familiar.

> "Good designers copy, great designers steal... the hex codes from Notion."

By pairing these colors with the beautiful **Geist** font and some subtle *framer-motion* fades, the UI comes alive.
